Official Rules — read before entering

1. No purchase necessary

NO PURCHASE OR PAYMENT OF ANY KIND IS NECESSARY TO ENTER OR WIN. A purchase or payment will not improve your chances of winning. Void where prohibited by law.

2. Sponsor

The Stawasz Group, 728 S Broad St, Philadelphia, PA 19146 — a real estate team at Keller Williams Empower (the “Sponsor”).

3. Eligibility

Open to legal residents of the United States who are 18 years of age or older at the time of entry. Attendance at the TSG Block Party is not required to enter or to win. Employees, agents and independent contractors of Sponsor and of Keller Williams Empower, and their immediate family and household members, are not eligible to win.

4. Entry period

Entries are accepted from the time this form is published until 6:15 PM Eastern Time on Saturday, September 19, 2026, when the winner is drawn. Your entry is complete as soon as you verify your email address. Bonus entries are added only when a referred person confirms (see section 5); a confirmation received after 6:15 PM does not add entries to the drawing.

5. How to enter — one entry, plus five per confirmed referral

To enter, submit this form with your full name, phone number and email address, accept these Official Rules, and verify your email address using the code we send you. That gives you one (1) entry in the drawing. Referring someone is optional and is not required to enter or to win.

To improve your chances, you may refer a friend or family member who is considering buying or selling a home within the next year, by giving their name, phone number and email address. We will email that person and ask them to confirm their details and give their own consent to be contacted. WHEN THEY CONFIRM, YOU RECEIVE FIVE (5) ADDITIONAL ENTRIES. If they decline, or do not respond before 6:15 PM on September 19, 2026, you receive no additional entries — your own entry still stands.

LIMIT ONE (1) ENTRY PER PERSON, PLUS FIVE (5) BONUS ENTRIES PER CONFIRMED REFERRAL. A given person can be referred only once: if two people refer the same person, the bonus entries go to whichever referral that person confirms first, and the other earns nothing. A person who is already a contact of Sponsor cannot be referred and does not earn bonus entries. There is no limit on how many different people you may refer, but each must be a different person, and you may not refer yourself.

Duplicate or conflicting referrals are matched by email address and by phone number. Automated, robotic, scripted or bulk entries are void.

All federal, state and local taxes on the prize are the sole responsibility of the winner.

7. Odds

Odds of winning depend on the total number of entries in the drawing, including bonus entries earned from confirmed referrals.

The winner need not be present to win. If the winner is not present at the announcement, Sponsor will attempt to notify the winner using the contact information provided. If the winner cannot be reached, declines the prize, is found ineligible, or fails to claim the prize within fourteen (14) days of the drawing, the prize may be forfeited and an alternate winner drawn at random from the remaining eligible entries.
